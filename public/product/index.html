A DAY INSIDE SHIMMERSTOCK

From Novi's morning brief to the end of the fulfillment queue.

This is what a typical operational day looks like when everything is connected. Not six separate tools. One workspace.

8:00
Novi

Morning Brief

Novi surfaces 3 things that matter today: 14 orders ready to ship, a Vanilla Base runway risk, and a customer conversation that's been waiting 18 hours.

8:15
Ship

Review fulfillment exceptions

Open the fulfillment queue. Order #8197 needs a label reprint. Clear it. The pack lane can now move uninterrupted.

NNovi flagged this before the team arrived. No manual check needed.

9:00
Stock

Receive incoming inventory

An inbound PO from your fragrance oil supplier arrives. Receive it against the purchase order. Inventory counts update. Committed stock recalculates.

10:30
Make

Start the production batch

Production Batch #52 kicks off. Component inventory auto-reserves against the batch recipe. The team works the queue. You can see progress without asking anyone.

NNovi noted that Formula 26 demand is up 28% — she'd suggest bumping the next batch by 40 units. Your decision.

1:00
Stock

Review the purchase recommendation

Novi has prepared a draft PO for Vanilla Base: 12 kg, ~$148, 9-day lead time. You review the numbers, the logic, the trade-offs. You approve. Nothing was sent until you said yes.

2:30
Care

Customer issue — with full context

A customer emails about a delayed order. You open the conversation. The full order history, fulfillment status, and tracking are already there. Your first reply is an actual answer.

NNovi surfaced this conversation because it had been waiting 18 hours. It was already in the morning brief.

4:30
Novi

Tomorrow's priorities

Before you close the day, Novi shows you what's ahead: a production batch that needs scheduling, two supplier POs to review, and a wholesale order wave coming Thursday.
