THE REAL STORY

Running a craft-supply and e-commerce business is operationally complex in a way most software ignores.

Product catalogs with thousands of SKUs across dozens of categories — fragrance oils, glitters, molds, blanks, packaging, vinyl, fabric, sublimation supplies. Each one with multiple sizes, weights, and variants. Each one with its own supplier, lead time, and restocking window.

Inventory pressure

When your best-selling fragrance oil is accelerating faster than the reorder is moving, you don't find out from a dashboard. You find out when a customer emails asking where their order is.

Order and fulfillment chaos

Hundreds of orders a day from multiple channels. Shopify. Wholesale. Live selling. Each order has different rules, different packing notes, different customer expectations.

The warehouse reality

Multiple locations. Bins. Receiving. Vendor POs. Products that arrive in bulk and get split into multiple sizes and configurations. None of this fits in a spreadsheet anymore.

Production and assembly

Kits. Bundles. Custom formula work. Batch production where the component inventory has to stay aligned with what's actually being made.

The teams

Packing teams. Customer-care roles. Purchasing teams. Each one needs different information, different permissions, and a way to coordinate without an owner-sized bottleneck.

The partners and affiliates

Brand reps. Wholesale accounts. Ambassador programs. All of this has to live somewhere, and that somewhere is usually a spreadsheet next to 11 other spreadsheets.

THE PROBLEM

Five apps. Three spreadsheets. One question nobody can answer.

Shopify for orders. A separate inventory tool for stock. A fulfillment tool for shipping. A support inbox that doesn't know what the order says. A batch spreadsheet on someone's laptop. A purchase order tracker that is also a spreadsheet. An affiliate tracker that is also a spreadsheet.

The question was always something like: How many days of Vanilla Base do we have left given current order volume and the batch running this week?

The answer lived in four different places, none of which talked to each other.

The realization

"Enterprise-level operational clarity should not require enterprise-level complexity. Small-business owners deserve to have this information without needing a team of analysts to compile it."

REAL VALIDATION

ShimmerStock is being built and validated against real craft-supply and e-commerce operations.

Not simulated. Not demo data. Real product catalogs, real purchase orders, real order flows, real team permissions, real customer histories.

Honest disclosure:

The full migration is in progress. Not complete yet. We're building it carefully, testing against real operational pressure, and shipping it when it's right. You'll see the real status — Live, Beta, Early Access, Planned — on every feature throughout the product.

A LETTER FROM THE FOUNDER

I know what it feels like to grow a business until the systems that once worked start fighting you.

There's a moment — and if you've run a growing product business, you've probably felt it — where you realize the spreadsheet you built three years ago is now the single most important document in your company, and it scares you. Because you don't fully trust it anymore, and neither does anyone else on your team.

I built ShimmerStock because I was living that reality. Running a craft-supply and e-commerce business. Managing thousands of SKUs, multiple locations, supplier relationships, production batches, fulfillment queues, customer care, wholesale, affiliates, and a team that needed actual information to do their jobs well.

The software that existed was either too simple, too expensive, too generic, or too disconnected. None of it understood the actual shape of a product business at the growth stage where the problems get real.

So I started building the thing I wanted to use. Not the thing that would look impressive in a pitch deck, but the thing that would actually make Tuesday mornings less chaotic.

That's ShimmerStock. It's being built carefully, with real operational data, and with the honesty to admit when something is still in progress. Every feature is labeled with its actual status. No fake functionality. No simulated customer counts. Just the real thing, getting better every week.

Pink is my own spark. Purple is for Dad, who taught me that calm steadiness is a superpower in business. Green is for Mom, who taught me that drive and humor can get you through anything. Navy and grey were our wedding colors — my husband keeps me grounded and tells me the truth when I need to hear it. Novi carries all of them.

If you're running a product business and you're tired of opening five apps to answer one question — this is for you.

Monica Founder, ShimmerStock
