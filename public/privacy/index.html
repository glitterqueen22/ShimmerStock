PRIVACY

Privacy Draft

Draft for Early Access - subject to legal review.

Data categories: account details, business profile details, authentication/session metadata, operational records (inventory, orders, purchasing, production, fulfillment, customer care), and public-site form submissions (Early Access and Dream Grant where enabled).

Purpose: provide, secure, and improve ShimmerStock; process onboarding requests; support customer communication and troubleshooting; maintain auditability and abuse prevention.

Service providers: infrastructure, logging, and communications providers needed to operate the service, under contractual safeguards.

Retention: retained only as needed for product operations, compliance, security, and legitimate business records, then deleted or de-identified based on policy and legal requirements.

Shopify/store data: in current Early Access, Shopify connection remains read-only beta and access is tenant-scoped.

Cookies and sessions: secure HttpOnly session cookies are used for authenticated app access. Public marketing pages may use minimal technical cookies/session mechanisms where required.

Early Access and Dream Grant submissions: used for application review and program communication; submissions may be disabled in private staging or when programs are closed.

Selling personal information: ShimmerStock does not sell personal information.

Access/deletion requests: contact privacy@shimmerstock.com or use Data Request.
