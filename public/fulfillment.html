What it does

Pick, pack, and ship — one continuous flow

ShimmerStock's Fulfillment & Shipping engine turns order fulfillment from a multi-tool scramble into one smooth, barcode-verified process. Orders flow from your commerce channels into a unified picking queue. Warehouse staff scan items as they pick, verifying each one against the order. At the packing station, packing recipes guide the process — every item, insert, and label is checked off. Carrier labels generate with one click. Tracking numbers post back automatically.

The platform also lets you combine orders going to the same address, split orders when items ship separately, and flag orders that need special handling. Every step is logged, so you always know exactly where an order is in the fulfillment pipeline.

Workflow

The fulfillment pipeline — orders never get stuck

1

Orders arrive in "To Pick"

Every order — Shopify, manual, wholesale — lands in a unified picking queue. Sort by priority, warehouse zone, or carrier deadline for maximum efficiency.

2

Scan as you pick

Warehouse staff scan each item's barcode. The system verifies the right SKU and quantity against the order. Any mismatch stops the process immediately, preventing errors.

3

Pack with a recipe

At the packing station, a packing recipe guides every step: items verified, inserts checked, packaging confirmed. Nothing gets into the box that shouldn't be there.

4

Ship and notify

Carrier label prints directly. Tracking number posts to the order automatically. Customer gets notified. Inventory updates. Done — all in one continuous flow.
