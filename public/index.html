FAQ

Questions you might have

What is ShimmerStock?

ShimmerStock is one platform for product-based businesses — inventory, orders, fulfillment, customers, partners, and growth. Novi, your business companion, watches everything so you never miss what matters.

How does Shopify integration work?

Connect your Shopify store with one click via OAuth. Products and orders sync automatically. By default, we use read-only access — you opt in to write permissions when you're ready.

Can I use ShimmerStock without Shopify?

Yes! You can create manual orders, add products by hand, and manage inventory without any integration. Shopify makes it faster, but it's not required.

What happens if I cancel?

Export your data anytime — no lock-in, no penalties. Your Shopify data stays in your Shopify account. We sync, we don't own.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — 10 days, full access, no credit card required. Start on any plan and see how ShimmerStock fits your business.

What is Novi?

Novi is your proactive business companion — not a chatbot. She watches inventory, orders, and opportunities and surfaces what needs your attention before you have to ask.

How is this different from Shopify's built-in tools?

Shopify is great at selling. ShimmerStock connects selling to operations — inventory, production, purchasing, and fulfillment in one place, with a companion who watches everything.

What about Etsy, Amazon, Faire?

Coming soon! Right now we support Shopify plus manual orders. Multi-channel integrations are on the roadmap.

Do I need technical skills?
