What it does

Multi-program partner management — one unified hub

ShimmerStock's Partner HQ lets you create and manage multiple partner programs simultaneously — wholesale accounts, affiliate marketers, and brand ambassadors — each with their own custom terms, commission or discount structures, and dedicated partner portals. Partners apply through branded application pages, and you approve or decline from a unified review screen. Once approved, partners get their own dashboard showing their sales, commissions, and performance metrics.

Sales are tracked automatically: when an affiliate's link drives a purchase, the commission accrues without a spreadsheet. When a wholesale partner places an order with their custom pricing, it flows into your unified order queue alongside DTC orders. Payout calculations, pending approvals, and performance reports are all surfaced proactively — no manual reconciliation, no forgotten payouts.

Workflow

Partner programs that run themselves

1

Create your program

Define the terms: wholesale with 20% off and net-30, affiliates with 10% commission, ambassadors with 15% discount code. Each program gets its own settings and branding.

2

Partners apply

Share your branded application page. Partners apply with their details, and applications land in your unified review queue — no emails to dig through.

Approve and onboard

Review and approve with one click. Partners get immediate access to their portal with custom pricing, unique tracking links, and performance dashboards.
