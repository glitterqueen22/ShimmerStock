SHOPIFY

Shopify Connection — Early Access / Read-only Beta

S
Shopify E-commerce platform
Read-only Beta

ShimmerStock currently connects to Shopify in read-only mode. No inventory writes, no order modifications, no mutations. The connection pulls the data your operations need and keeps it synchronized without touching your live Shopify store.

Current scopes — exactly these four, no more

read_orders
read_products
read_inventory
read_locations

What ShimmerStock reads from Shopify

  • Products, variants, and SKU details
  • Orders and order line items
  • Inventory levels per location
  • Fulfillment locations

What ShimmerStock never writes to Shopify

  • No inventory adjustments
  • No order modifications or cancellations
  • No product or variant changes
  • No fulfillments or tracking updates
  • No refunds, no webhooks without approval

Write scope requires a separate owner-approved milestone and will be announced transparently.

Data ownership and security

  • Shopify access tokens are encrypted at rest
  • Connection is tenant-scoped — no cross-business data access
  • Disconnect at any time from the Commerce settings page
  • GGE and real merchant stores remain disconnected until a separate pilot milestone
