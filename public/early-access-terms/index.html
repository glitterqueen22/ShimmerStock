EARLY ACCESS TERMS

Early Access Program Terms Draft

Draft for Early Access - subject to legal review.

Beta nature: Early Access is an evolving program and includes pre-general-availability workflows.

Program changes: features, plan packaging, onboarding flow, and rollout priorities may change.

Feedback: participants may provide product feedback that helps shape roadmap and operational workflows.

Pricing lock: introductory base pricing lock applies while continuously subscribed under active program terms.

No guaranteed feature timeline: roadmap items labeled Planned or Demo do not have guaranteed release dates.

Data and testing expectations: participants should use accurate business data responsibly and verify critical outputs before action.

Termination: Early Access participation may be paused or ended by either party in accordance with these terms.
