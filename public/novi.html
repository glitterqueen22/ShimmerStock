What she does

Proactive intelligence that never sleeps

Novi is the intelligence layer that watches every ShimmerStock engine simultaneously. She doesn't wait for you to ask — she tells you what needs your attention. Low inventory? She spots it before you run out. Delayed shipment? She flags it. Two orders going to the same address? She suggests combining them. A customer who hasn't ordered in 90 days? She drafts a re-engagement email.

But Novi isn't just about problems. She celebrates your wins. First sale? She's there. Record revenue month? She notices. 100th order shipped? She's already drafting a celebration message. Every morning, she delivers a personalized briefing — what happened yesterday, what needs attention today, and what opportunities are on the horizon. She's the teammate who never sleeps, never judges, and never stops believing in your dream.
