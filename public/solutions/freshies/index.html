THE OPERATIONAL REALITY

Freshie businesses are more operationally complex than they look.

Formula management

Multiple scents. Multiple load percentages. Aroma bead quantities that need to reconcile with available fragrance inventory. A formula that works at 12% doesn't work the same way if you're out of oil.

Batch production

Batches that depend on multiple components arriving, being mixed correctly, and curing before they go to pack. The sequencing matters and the batch notes matter even more.

Blank and mold inventory

Dozens of mold shapes and blank variations. Some popular. Some seasonal. Some with lead times that punish a late reorder. All of them need to stay in sync with the production schedule.

Bundle and kit complexity

Bundle packs that require multiple freshie variants with different curing schedules. Shipping windows that depend on the slowest component being ready.

Wholesale and live selling

A live sale on Friday can change the inventory picture for the rest of the month. Wholesale orders that need to be committed against available stock without double-selling.

The spreadsheet problem

Most freshie businesses hit a wall where the production spreadsheet, the order spreadsheet, and the inventory spreadsheet stop agreeing with each other. That's when things get expensive.
