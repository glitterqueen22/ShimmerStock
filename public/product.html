ShimmerStock replaces the patchwork of tools, spreadsheets, and browser tabs that run your product business. Explore every engine — from inventory to fulfillment to partners to growth — all designed to work together from day one.

What it does

Every engine. One platform. Zero dead ends.

ShimmerStock is built around the Operating Loop™ — nine purpose-built engines that hand off work to each other in a continuous flow. When an order comes in from Shopify, inventory adjusts automatically. When a production batch completes, finished goods are added to stock and raw materials are consumed. When a partner sale happens, commissions accrue without a spreadsheet.

At the center of it all is Novi, the proactive business companion who watches every engine simultaneously. She surfaces what needs attention, drafts what you need, and celebrates every milestone like she owns the place.

No more logging into five different platforms. No more cross-referencing spreadsheets against your Shopify dashboard. No more wondering if something fell through the cracks. ShimmerStock connects every part of your product business and keeps it all moving.

How it works

The Operating Loop™ — every action hands off to the next

1

Commerce

Orders flow in from Shopify — or you create them manually. Every order is visible, searchable, and actionable from one screen.

2

Inventory

Stock levels update automatically. Reorder points trigger alerts. Warehouse views show exactly what's where.

3

Production

When you manufacture a batch, finished goods are added to inventory and raw materials are consumed — no manual adjustments.

4

Purchasing

Low-stock alerts from Inventory generate purchase order recommendations. Suppliers, lead times, and costs are tracked.

5

Fulfillment

Orders move through pick → pack → ship in one barcode-verified flow. Labels print without leaving the platform.

6

Customer Care

Every conversation is tied to order history. Novi drafts replies. Returns and replacements happen in-context.
