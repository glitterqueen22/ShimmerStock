What it does

Live inventory visibility across every location

ShimmerStock's Inventory & Warehouse engine gives you live visibility into every product across every location. It tracks stock levels in real time — synced from Shopify, purchase orders, production batches, and manual adjustments. You can set reorder points per product, organize inventory into bins and warehouses, and view stock history at a glance. When inventory moves, the platform updates instantly — no lag, no manual reconciliation.

Stock-level views show you exactly what's in stock, available to sell, reserved for orders, incoming from purchase orders, and committed to production batches. Every transaction is logged with who did what and when — complete traceability from raw material to shipped order.

Workflow

Inventory at the center of the Operating Loop™

1

Orders deduct automatically

When an order comes in — from Shopify, wholesale, or manual entry — inventory is automatically deducted. Available quantities update instantly across every warehouse view.

2

Purchase orders replenish

When a purchase order is received, stock levels update. Incoming inventory appears in your warehouse view before it physically arrives so you can plan ahead.

3

Production batches flow through

When a production batch completes, finished goods are added to inventory and raw materials are consumed — all in one continuous flow. No manual spreadsheets needed.

4

Novi watches continuously

Low stock? Supplier lead time approaching? Seasonal demand spike? Novi surfaces what you need to know before it becomes a problem — not after you've already run out.
