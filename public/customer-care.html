What it does

One inbox. Every conversation. Full order context.

ShimmerStock's Customer Care engine replaces the fragmented support experience with a unified inbox that knows everything about every customer. When an email arrives, the conversation is automatically linked to the customer's order history. You see every order they've placed, every return they've initiated, every message they've sent — all in one view. Novi drafts replies based on the full context, and you can review and send with one click.

Customers get a self-service portal where they can track orders, initiate returns, check store credit balances, and manage their account. For your team, automation rules handle common scenarios — damaged items, address changes, shipping inquiries — routing them to the right person with pre-drafted responses. Every interaction is logged, so nothing falls through the cracks and no customer has to repeat themselves.

The result: faster responses, happier customers, and a support experience that feels personal instead of transactional.

Workflow

Support that flows with your business

1

Customer reaches out

Email arrives in the unified inbox. The system automatically links it to the customer's profile and shows their full order history, past conversations, and any open returns.

2

Novi drafts a response

With full context — what they ordered, when it shipped, past issues — Novi drafts a personalized reply. You review, edit if needed, and send. Or let automation handle common scenarios.

3

Resolve in context

Need to issue a return? Create a replacement order? Add store credit? All from within the conversation — no switching tools, no copy-pasting order numbers between systems.

4

Customer self-serves

The customer portal lets people track orders, initiate returns, check store credit, and view their history — reducing inbound tickets and giving customers the autonomy they want.
