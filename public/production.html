What it does

Production management built for makers

ShimmerStock's Production engine gives makers and manufacturers a dedicated workspace for managing recipes (BOMs), manufacturing batches, raw material consumption, and cost tracking. Create a recipe once — define every raw material, quantity, and step — and it's versioned and documented forever. When you start a production batch, materials are reserved from inventory. Record yields as the batch progresses. When it's complete, finished goods are automatically added to inventory and raw materials are consumed — one continuous flow, no manual adjustments.

Track actual vs. expected yields. Monitor per-unit costs. See which batches are in progress, which are scheduled, and which raw materials are running low. The production schedule integrates with your purchasing intelligence — when a recipe calls for materials that are below reorder point, Novi flags it before you start the batch, not after you realize you're short.

Workflow

Production that flows directly into inventory

1

Create your recipe (BOM)

Define every raw material, quantity per unit, and production step. Recipes are versioned — update a formula and the old version is preserved for reference.

2

Start a production batch

Select a recipe, set the batch size, and start. Raw materials are reserved from inventory. Novi warns you if any materials are below what's needed for the full batch.

3

Record yields as you go

Track actual output vs. expected. If a batch yields 48 instead of 50, record it — costs update automatically. Quality notes and lot numbers are captured in real time.

4

Complete the batch

Finished goods are added to inventory. Raw materials are consumed. Per-unit costs are calculated. The batch is documented and archived — ready for your next production run.
