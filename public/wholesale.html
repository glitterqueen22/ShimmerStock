What it does

Wholesale built into your operations — not bolted on

ShimmerStock's wholesale engine isn't a separate platform or a third-party marketplace — it's built directly into your operating system. You create tiered price lists, set minimum order quantities, configure net payment terms, and invite wholesale partners to their own branded portal. They browse your catalog at wholesale prices, place bulk orders, and track their shipments — all without ever emailing you for a price list.

Because wholesale shares the same inventory pool as your DTC business, you never oversell. A wholesale order deducts from the same stock levels as a Shopify order — one source of truth. Invoices are generated automatically with the terms you set. And when a wholesale partner needs to reorder, Novi notices the pattern and can even draft a friendly reorder reminder.

"Let me send you the wholesale price list" — retired forever

Invoices generate automatically with the terms you set. And because everything shares the same inventory, you never sell stock you don't have.

Workflow

Wholesale that runs alongside your DTC business

1

Create pricing tiers

Set up tiered price lists — Gold at 40% off, Silver at 30% off, Bronze at 20% off — with minimum order quantities and net payment terms for each tier.

2

Invite wholesale partners

Send invitations to your retail partners. They get their own branded portal where they see your catalog at their pricing tier and can place orders anytime.

3

Orders flow into your unified queue

Wholesale orders appear alongside your DTC orders — same queue, same fulfillment flow, same inventory pool. No separate system to check or reconcile.

4

Invoices and payments, automated

Invoices generate automatically with the terms you set. Partners can view their order history and invoice status from their portal — no more chasing payment statuses.
